AI Evaluation & Scoring Fidelity

How our specialized neural models emulate strict examiners and examiners’ marking keys.

Unlike generic language models, SuperKalam decomposes your handwritten response through a four-stage assessment pipeline:

  • Syllabus & Command Word Alignment: Checks if your answer directly addresses directives like "Critically Analyze", "Elucidate", or "Evaluate".
  • Structural & Dimension Auditing: Evaluates your Introduction, Thematic Arguments (PESTLE framework: Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental), and crisp Forward-looking Conclusion.
  • Factual & Constitutional Precision: Flags inaccuracies in referenced Constitution Articles (e.g. 1973 Constitution or Indian Constitution), landmark supreme court verdicts, and official committee statistics.
  • Penalty Engine: Applies realistic FPSC/UPSC negative marking rules for unverified assertions, irrelevant digressions, and grammatical blunders.

Handwriting OCR & Mobile Scanner

Uploading your real paper copies using your smartphone camera or PDF document scanner.

SuperKalam’s OCR was custom-trained on over 180,000 real civil service candidate scripts, specifically encompassing rapid examination penmanship, cursive scripts, fountain pen inks, and ballpoint pressure variations.

Even if your handwriting feels hurried or untidy, our dual-pass context-aware decoder reconstructs words based on contextual civil service terminology. In the rare event a word is genuinely unintelligible, it highlights the exact word and alerts you that a human examiner would also dock marks for poor legibility.

For the fastest and most flawless OCR reading, we recommend:

1. Diffused Light Ensure natural overhead light without severe hand shadows falling on the page.
2. Top-Down Angle Hold the phone parallel to the desk so page margins do not taper off perspectively.
3. Batch Multi-page Use our in-browser camera tool or upload a clean multi-page PDF directly.
